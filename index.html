01

About

I am a Brazilian mathematician working in probability theory, with an emphasis on stochastic processes and asymptotic phenomena.

I received my PhD in Mathematics from the Federal University of Bahia (UFBA) in 2025. My doctoral work investigates convergence of Feller processes through their generators, with applications to functional central limit theorems for boundary random walks and Brownian-type processes.

Most recently, I carried out postdoctoral research at the University of Augsburg, where I also worked on dynamic percolation and evasion problems involving Brownian regions.

02

Research

My research sits at the interface of discrete and continuous probability: I study how microscopic random systems produce macroscopic stochastic behavior.

A

Scaling limits

Weak convergence of discrete stochastic models to continuous processes, with quantitative estimates whenever possible.

B

Boundary random walks

Random walks on the half-line and the role of boundary mechanisms in producing different Brownian-type limits.

C

Feller processes

Generator-based methods for convergence in distribution, including Trotter–Kato type results.

D

Dynamic percolation

Evasion and connectivity questions in evolving random environments, including Brownian regions.
