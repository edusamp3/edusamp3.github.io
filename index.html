Mathematician · Probability

Eduardo Sampaio Pimenta

Mathematician working in probability theory

I am a Brazilian mathematician interested in stochastic processes, scaling limits and random walks. I completed my PhD in Mathematics at the Federal University of Bahia (UFBA) in 2025 and most recently carried out postdoctoral research at the University of Augsburg.

My work studies how discrete random systems converge to continuous processes and how boundary mechanisms affect their limiting behavior.

Research

My main interests are in probability theory and stochastic processes, especially the following topics:

  • scaling limits and invariance principles;
  • random walks with boundary mechanisms;
  • Feller processes and generator methods;
  • dynamic percolation and evolving random environments.
