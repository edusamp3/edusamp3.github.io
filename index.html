Eduardo Sampaio Pimenta

Mathematician working in probability theory

I am a Brazilian mathematician interested in stochastic processes, scaling limits and random walks. I completed my PhD in Mathematics at the Federal University of Bahia (UFBA) in 2025 and most recently carried out postdoctoral research at the University of Augsburg. I have been a postdoctoral researcher at the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG) since October 2026.

My work studies how discrete random systems converge to continuous processes and how boundary mechanisms affect their limiting behavior.
