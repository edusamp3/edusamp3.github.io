02

Probability Theory

Research

I study how discrete stochastic models converge to continuous processes and how boundaries shape their limiting behavior.

A

Scaling limits

Weak convergence of discrete stochastic models to continuous processes, with quantitative estimates whenever possible.

B

Boundary random walks

Random walks on the half-line and the role of boundary mechanisms in producing different Brownian-type limits.

C

Feller processes

Generator-based methods for convergence in distribution, including Trotter–Kato type results.

D

Dynamic percolation

Evasion and connectivity questions in evolving random environments, including Brownian regions.

Guiding question

Which microscopic mechanisms survive under scaling, and how do they appear in the generator and boundary behavior of the limiting process?
