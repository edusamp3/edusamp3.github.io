About

A mathematician interested in the passage from discrete random systems to continuous stochastic behavior.

I am a Brazilian mathematician working in probability theory, with an emphasis on stochastic processes and asymptotic phenomena.

I received my PhD in Mathematics from the Federal University of Bahia (UFBA) in 2025. My doctoral work investigates convergence of Feller processes through their generators, with applications to functional central limit theorems for boundary random walks and Brownian-type processes.

Most recently, I carried out postdoctoral research at the University of Augsburg. There I also worked on dynamic percolation and evasion problems involving Brownian regions.

My broader interests include scaling limits, invariance principles, random walks, interacting systems and probabilistic models in evolving environments.

Focus

Understanding how local random mechanisms create universal behavior at large scales.
